Privacy policy

Effective 9 October 2026

Funnier is a humor practice app for iPhone. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: there are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, your practice history stays on your device, and a conversation excerpt only leaves your phone when you ask for AI feedback on it.

Information stays on your device

When you save a practice moment, Funnier stores your reply, its feedback, the date, your XP and a title that may contain words from the conversation. It keeps the latest 500 attempts plus overall progress totals. This data is stored only on your device. It is not encrypted by Funnier, not synced between devices, and not sent to us.

Chats you import (a WhatsApp .txt export, an Instagram messages .json export, or pasted text) are read on your device and kept in memory only for the current session. Closing the app removes them. Your original files are never changed.

When you ask for AI feedback

The first time you ask for AI feedback, Funnier asks your permission and remembers your answer on your device. You can withdraw it any time in Privacy & help. With your permission, each review sends the selected conversation excerpt (at most 12 messages), the practice prompt and your reply over an encrypted connection to Funnier's coaching service. The service replaces participants' names with labels such as "Person 1" and passes the text to OpenAI, which generates the feedback. Message text itself can still contain names or other details, so avoid sharing sensitive information about yourself or others.

The coaching service does not store your conversations, replies or feedback, and does not write them to logs. To prevent abuse and control costs, it keeps an anonymous session token (stored only as a hash), request counters keyed by a salted hash of your IP address (never the raw IP), and token usage and cost records. These records contain no chat content.

OpenAI processes requests under its API data policies. Funnier asks OpenAI not to store responses for later use. OpenAI may still retain API data for a limited period for abuse and misuse monitoring, as its policies describe. OpenAI does not use API data to train its models by default.

The coaching service runs on Railway infrastructure.

What we don't do

  • No account, sign-in or contact details are required or collected.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share your information for advertising.
  • Funnier never connects to, signs in to or reads your WhatsApp or Instagram account.

Your choices and deletion

You decide whether excerpts are sent for AI feedback, and can withdraw that permission in Privacy & help. Clear chats on the Chats screen removes imported moments. Clear saved practice history on the Progress screen deletes your saved attempts, XP and progress from your device. Deleting the app also deletes its local data. Because the coaching service doesn't keep your chat content, there is nothing for it to delete.

Children

Funnier is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue on Funnier's support page on GitHub, or see Support.

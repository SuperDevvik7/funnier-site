Support

Need help with Funnier, found a bug, or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub and we'll get back to you. Please don't include private conversations in an issue.

How does Funnier work?

Pick a moment from Today, Practice or your own chats. Read the conversation, write your punchline, and ask for feedback. You'll get a score for surprise, relevance, originality and brevity, an honest take on what works and what doesn't, and three funnier lines. Then try again or move on to the next moment.

Why is the feedback so blunt?

On purpose. The coach judges your reply the way the chat would react, so a 4 means it wouldn't land yet. It's harsh about the joke, never about you.

How do I practice with my own chats?

On the Your chats tab, choose a WhatsApp chat exported without media (.txt) or an Instagram messages export (.json), or paste a few lines in "Name: message" format. Tell Funnier which person is you, and it finds moments where you could have replied.

AI feedback isn't working

Check your connection and try again. Your reply is always kept. If you see a message that the coach has reached its monthly limit, AI feedback will be available again next month.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Progress, then Clear saved practice history. Imported chats disappear when you close the app, or use Clear chats. See the privacy policy for details.
